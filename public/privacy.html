ParkEase Pune — Privacy Policy
Operated by Aenrox | Effective Date: November 25, 2026

ParkEase ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and operated by Aenrox, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data collected through the ParkEase Android application (com.aenrox.parkease) and web platform (parkease.aenrox.com).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, and mobile phone number provided during registration to manage reservations, host payouts, and access authentication.
  • Vehicle & Reservation Details: Vehicle license plate numbers and reservation date/time windows required solely to issue Society Gate Security Authorization Passes and verify entry with society security personnel.
  • Location Information (Optional / Approximate & Precise): Requested only when using the interactive map to locate nearby parking spaces in Pune. Location data is used transiently in-app and is not sold to third parties.
  • Payment Information: Payments are processed securely via Indian payment gateways (Razorpay / UPI). ParkEase does not store raw debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • Facilitating peer-to-peer parking space reservations, gate security QR verification, and host settlement accounting (80% net host payout / 20% platform fee).
  • Preventing double-booking conflicts and unauthorized bay access.
  • Customer support and transaction audit trails.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We share necessary booking details (commuter name, phone number, and vehicle license number) exclusively with the corresponding parking space host/housing society gate for access verification, and payment parameters with Razorpay / UPI apps for checkout execution. We never sell user data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Security & Data Retention

All traffic between the ParkEase Android app and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Users may request account and data deletion at any time by contacting support.
